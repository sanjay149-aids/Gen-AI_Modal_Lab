1. Zero-shot Prompting:
Prompt:
Meena has ₹500. She buys a book for ₹180 and a bag for ₹250. How much money is left?

Output:
Start with the total money: ₹500

Subtract the cost of the book:
₹500 − ₹180 = ₹320

Now subtract the cost of the bag:
₹320 − ₹250 = ₹70

Final Answer: ₹70 left


2. One-shot Prompting:
Prompt:
Example:
Ravi has ₹300. He spends ₹100 and ₹50. Money left = 300 - 100 - 50 = ₹150.

Now solve:
Meena has ₹500. She buys a book for ₹180 and a bag for ₹250. How much money is left?

Output:
Money left = 500 - 180 - 250
= 320 - 250
= ₹70


3. Few-shot Prompting:
Prompt:
Example 1:
A has ₹400, spends ₹100 and ₹50 → left = ₹250

Example 2:
B has ₹600, spends ₹200 and ₹150 → left = ₹250

Now solve:
Meena has ₹500. She buys a book for ₹180 and a bag for ₹250. How much money is left?

Output:
Meena has ₹500, spends ₹180 and ₹250

Money left = 500 - 180 - 250
= 320 - 250
= ₹70



4. Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting:
Prompt:
Meena has ₹500. She buys a book for ₹180 and a bag for ₹250.
Let’s solve step by step.

Reasoning:

Step 1: Total spent = 180 + 250 = 430
Step 2: Remaining money = 500 - 430 = 70

Output:
Your step-by-step reasoning is correct.

Step 1: Total spent = 180 + 250 = 430
Step 2: Remaining money = 500 - 430 = 70

Final Answer: ₹70
